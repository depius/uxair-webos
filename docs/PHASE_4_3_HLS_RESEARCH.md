# Phase 4.3 — Advanced HLS research

UxAir Direct HLS downloads a bounded clear VOD playlist, picks an initial compatible variant, demuxes muxed tracks, and feeds compressed video and stereo PCM to Starfish BUFFERSTREAM. AirPlay admission and encrypted playback controls use a separate connection protocol. The LG B7 baseline confirms a master playlist and VOD seeks. All extensions below need their own acceptance tests.

| Feature | Current behavior and required work | B7 constraints and risks | Priority and complexity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WebVTT subtitles | The direct player ignores the subtitle rendition group and renders no cues. Resolve `EXT-X-MEDIA:TYPE=SUBTITLES` against the selected variant. Parse segmented WebVTT and `X-TIMESTAMP-MAP`, unwrap MPEG-TS time, retain active cues across segment edges, and clear them on seek, replacement and EOS. | BUFFERSTREAM receives video and PCM. A receiver-owned overlay needs a verified way to composite over the TV video plane. Test legibility, safe areas, cue overlap and rendering during pause. Safari selection signaling is unverified. | Medium, medium |
| Alternate audio and language | The initial selector accepts embedded audio. A variant requiring a separate audio URI is excluded. Resolve its rendition group, apply `DEFAULT`, `AUTOSELECT`, `LANGUAGE` and user preference, and fetch and align separate audio segments. An in-flight language switch needs a PCM clock transition and a verified sender or local UI control. | PCM keeps codec choice independent of the Starfish audio path. Separate download and preroll budgets must hold on ARMv7. Misaligned segment starts, differing timestamps and channel-layout changes can produce gaps. | High, high |
| Adaptive bitrate during playback | Master selection and one pre-start fallback exist. Maintain an eligible variant set and use measured throughput with buffer depth. Align target segments at a verified random-access boundary with compatible timelines, maps and codec parameters. | A resolution or codec change may require unloading and reopening BUFFERSTREAM. The B7 picture gap and A/V drift during a switch are unknown. Start with video variants of the same codec and geometry and measure before expanding. | Medium, high |
| Live HLS and refresh | The direct parser requires `ENDLIST`. Add bounded reloads based on target duration, sequence and discontinuity tracking, a sliding seekable window, live-edge startup and endlist transition. Late segments and transient HTTP failures need separate recovery from permanent disappearance. | Long sessions exercise allocation and thread lifetime. The receiver needs a bounded buffer and credible seekable ranges in AirPlay state. Test playlist rollback, changing init maps, cancellation, and movement beyond an expired window. | Medium, high |
| Low-latency HLS | Partial segments, preload hints and live reload are outside the current parser and fetch loop. Apple LL-HLS needs blocking reload, partial media, rendition reports, server control, and coordination with the parent segment to avoid double feed. | Current fetch expects a complete fragment and native preroll before presentation. Partial networking alone gives no end-to-end latency guarantee. Measure Starfish preroll and transport cancellation on the B7 after ordinary live works. | Deferred, very high |
| Seek across discontinuities | The VOD path tracks declared periods and init-map changes and reopens BUFFERSTREAM at the boundary. Host fixtures cover period changes and existing seek behavior. Extend validation to seeking on both sides of a discontinuity with a PTS reset, changed map and changed audio codec. | A fresh decoder needs an independent random-access picture and parameter sets. Pending download, UI feed and PCM clocks must all retire under the correct generation. Confirm seek feedback and A/V alignment on the B7. | High, medium |
| AirPlay state synchronization | The existing encrypted routes accept sender rate and scrub commands, expose playback state, and send reverse events with an outstanding ACK. Capture per-connection event/ACK order and correlate it with loaded and seekable ranges, user commands and generation transitions. Validate callback timing with a real sender before altering reported state. | The wire semantics are based on interop evidence. The Phase 4 trace shows explicit Pause commands, and some ACKs arrive without a pending event. Treat valid Pause as authoritative and leave authentication and framing rules intact while collecting evidence. | High, medium for observability and fixtures |
| DRM | The parser rejects encrypted playlists and the demuxer rejects encrypted boxes and packet side data. FairPlay Streaming requires its authorized client, key exchange, license policy and a protected decode/output path. AirPlay authentication secures controls. | No supported protected-media Starfish integration is established. Keep the reject path and scope new features to clear fixtures. | Deferred, blocked on a supported licensed playback path |

Prioritize clear VOD seek/state fixtures, followed by alternate audio and language selection. Measure independent changes to subtitles, ABR and live delivery before combining them. LL-HLS depends on a reliable live path and measured decoder startup latency.

Protocol references [RFC 8216](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8216), [Apple HLS authoring specification](https://developer.apple.com/streaming/hls-authoring-specification-for-apple-devices.html), [Apple Low-Latency HLS](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/http-live-streaming/enabling-low-latency-http-live-streaming-hls), [Apple FairPlay Streaming](https://developer.apple.com/streaming/fps/).
